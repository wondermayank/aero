Yes. The site includes a built-in Canvas Wallpaper Editor. Open any wallpaper, click "Edit Wallpaper", choose 9:16 (Phone), and enable "Blurred Fit". This creates a phone-optimized wallpaper with a blurred ambient backdrop of the desktop wallpaper, fitted for mobile screens.
